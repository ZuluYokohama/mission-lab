# Independent V&V campaign — proposed, not implemented

Verify whether implementation conforms to the reviewed contract; separately validate whether that contract serves the researcher's intended use. Freeze a source identity, reviewed requirements, test population and pass/fail rules before final assessment. The public preview's existing validation is reported separately in VALIDATION.md.

1. Build an independent checker that imports no production planner/executor/report code. Check strict field types, graph dependencies, ownership, resource occupancy, events, raw/staged/semantic consistency and parent-to-revision differences. Link process measurements through independently observed launch/exit records.
2. Exhaust small finite state models for 1–2 input DAGs, 1–2 workers and bounded durations/resources. State liveness assumptions explicitly. Use a separate exact schedule oracle for 24 registered and 24 stress cases with at most seven nodes; cross-check 12 cases with at most four nodes. A 100,000-state or two-second oracle cap gives an inconclusive result, not a pass.
3. Compare hashes and byte counts with external known vectors and an independent provider. Include chunk boundaries, empty input, changed inputs, mismatched versions and deliberately incorrect results. Use metamorphic relations and demonstrate rejection of critical seeded faults.
4. Exercise eight fault families in disposable owned fixtures with an external watchdog: input mutation, output corruption, missing evidence, malformed/resealed records, worker failure, hang, dependency failure and exceptional cleanup. Preserve failures and partial evidence. Observe resource limits outside the producer's own event log.
5. Calibrate costs on sizes 0, 4 KiB, 32 KiB, 1 MiB and 16 MiB for three candidates with one warmup and five repetitions. Hold out 128 KiB and 4 MiB with three repetitions. Compare end-to-end boundaries including startup and mandatory verification. Preregister prediction gates (25% median, 50% p90 relative error) and treatment of zero-duration estimates.
6. Compare with equivalent sequential and fixed-parallel baselines using the same required checks, ten paired repetitions and a preregistered 10% practical improvement threshold plus uncertainty. Failure to demonstrate a benefit is an acceptable result.
7. Have a human review requirement interpretation, exclusions and outcomes. Require a second independent human before reporting inter-reviewer reliability. Retain unresolved findings and alternate explanations.

Acceptance requires traceable contract coverage, successful detection of critical faults, deterministic offline regeneration, retained failures, explicit measured resource boundaries, and preservation of prior evidence. Completion of this campaign must not be inferred from the present fixture demonstration.
